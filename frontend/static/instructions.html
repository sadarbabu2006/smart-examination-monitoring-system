Examination Guidelines & Code of Conduct

Review proctoring policies and system requirements before entering the examination.

Session Verification
Webcam Permission & Continuous Visibility
You must grant camera permissions on the next screen and keep your face clearly visible within the camera frame at all times. Stepping away or covering the camera will log an absence period.
Active Window & Tab Retention
You must keep the examination tab focused. Switching to another browser tab, opening new windows, or minimizing the browser will be automatically flagged and recorded as a proctoring violation.
Single Candidate Policy
Only the authenticated candidate is allowed in the testing environment. Detection of multiple faces or unauthorized individuals in the camera feed will be recorded in the session integrity audit.
Automated Integrity Analysis
All telemetry signals are securely analyzed to generate an examination integrity report for review by proctoring authorities upon session completion.
