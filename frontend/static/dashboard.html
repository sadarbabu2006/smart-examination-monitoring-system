Important Instructions

1
Keep your face clearly visible to the webcam throughout the examination session.
2
Do not switch browser tabs or minimize the testing window during active monitoring.
3
Do not leave the camera frame; absence intervals are automatically audited.
4
Ensure a well-lit, quiet room with a stable high-speed internet connection.
5
Follow all examination rules and academic integrity guidelines.

1. Before You Begin

1
Make sure your candidate information is correct.
2
Use a supported desktop or laptop browser.
3
Ensure your internet connection is stable.
4
Make sure your device has sufficient battery or is connected to power.
5
Choose a quiet and well-lit environment.
6
Make sure your face remains clearly visible to the webcam.
7
Close unnecessary applications, browser tabs, downloads, and notifications.
8
Complete the system check before starting the examination.

2. During the Examination

1
Remain present in front of the webcam throughout the examination.
2
Do not switch browser tabs or windows unnecessarily.
3
Do not minimize the examination window.
4
Do not leave the examination environment without authorization.
5
Follow all examination rules and academic integrity requirements.
6
Answer the examination independently.
7
Do not use unauthorized external resources or assistance.
8
Keep the examination window open until the examination is submitted.

3. Camera & System Requirements

•
Camera access is required for monitored examinations.
•
Allow camera permission when requested by the browser.
•
Use a supported modern browser.
•
Maintain a stable internet connection.
•
Complete the System Check before starting the examination.
•
Resolve any failed system check before beginning.

4. Academic Integrity

•
Complete the examination honestly and independently.
•
Do not use unauthorized notes, websites, applications, devices, or assistance.
•
Do not communicate with other people during the examination.
•
Do not attempt to bypass or interfere with examination monitoring.
•
Suspicious activity may be recorded and reviewed according to the applicable examination rules.
•
Follow any additional rules provided by the examination administrator or institution.

5. Submitting Your Examination

•
Review your answers before submitting when time permits.
•
Use the official Submit/Finish action provided by the examination interface.
•
Do not close the browser before submission is confirmed.
•
Wait for confirmation that the examination has been successfully submitted.
•
If a technical problem occurs, follow the designated support procedure.

6. Technical Issues & Troubleshooting

Camera not working:

Check browser camera permissions and make sure the camera is available.

Browser compatibility issue:

Use a supported modern browser.

Internet connection issue:

Restore a stable connection before continuing.

Unexpected page behavior:

Avoid repeatedly refreshing the examination page and contact support if necessary.

Issue during an active examination:

Follow the examination administrator's designated support procedure.
